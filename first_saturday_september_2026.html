First Saturday / September 2026

 

Our first regular Cornafulla Micro Reserve First Saturday brought people together at Cornafulla Bog to learn how to use spotting scopes and record wildlife observations on the National Biodiversity Data Centre. We also discussed the Merlin app developed by the Cornell Lab of Ornithology, the Seek app developed by iNaturalist and ObsIdentify developed by Observation.org Biodiversity Citizen Science & Monitoring
